## Algorithm
1. Start the program.
2. Enter the warehouse locations and initialize the adjacency list.
3. Enter the aisle connections and store each edge in both adjacency lists to represent an undirected graph.
4. Display the original graph with its adjacent locations.
5. Insert a new warehouse location (vertex) and add its associated aisle connections.
6. Display the updated graph after vertex insertion.
7. Select an existing aisle connection to remove.
8. Remove the selected edge from both adjacency lists.
9. Display the updated graph after edge deletion.
10. Select a warehouse location to examine.
11. Calculate and display the degree of the selected vertex or display its direct neighbours.
12. Stop the program.
